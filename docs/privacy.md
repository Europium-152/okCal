# okCal Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 September 2026_

okCal is a free, open-source calorie and weight tracker. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers of its own. The developer does not collect, receive, or store any of your data.

## Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (food logs, weights, recipes, goals, settings) is stored locally on your phone. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Use Export / Import in the app to back up or move your data.

## Optional features that contact third parties

**Open Food Facts (barcode scan and food search).** When you scan a barcode or search for a food online, the barcode or search text is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to fetch nutrition information. No account or personal identifier is sent. See their privacy policy at https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

**OpenAI (AI food logging, optional).** If you choose to use AI logging, you provide your own OpenAI API key in Settings. The key is stored only on your device. When you use the feature, your text description or food photo is sent directly from your phone to OpenAI to estimate calories. This goes straight to OpenAI, not through any server of ours. OpenAI's handling of that data is governed by their privacy policy at https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy. If you never add an API key, nothing is sent to OpenAI.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** to scan barcodes and to take photos of food for AI estimation. Photos are only sent to OpenAI if you use AI logging.
- **Photo library:** only when you pick a food photo for AI estimation.

## Children

okCal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
